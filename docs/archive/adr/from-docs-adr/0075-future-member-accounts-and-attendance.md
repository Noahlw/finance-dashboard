# Member accounts and attendance are future landing-page capabilities

Status: accepted

After the committee-only v1, the web app may become a landing page where members create app-managed accounts and sign in through the same application as Committee and Treasurer users without requiring Google accounts. Role Promotion remains spreadsheet-only: the web app does not grant or revoke privileged roles. After a spreadsheet role change, the promoted user completes a separate Google step-up sign-in; ordinary members may access an Attendance UI and scan an attendance QR code. These capabilities are future scope and must not weaken the v1 Users-sheet allowlist or committee-only finance boundary.
