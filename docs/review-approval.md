## Review & Approval

This domain covers verification, rejection, and correction workflows.

### Key decisions

- No self-verification (operator cannot verify own claims)
- Committee or Treasurer may reject
- Needs Info path with Discord notice to configured role
- Discord delivery does not block workflow
- Authorized operators see full payment details
- Full audit history for authorized operators
- Shared committee queues across all finance workflows
