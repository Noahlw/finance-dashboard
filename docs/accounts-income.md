## Accounts & Income

This domain covers finance accounts, income recording, and balance movements.

### Key decisions

- Finance accounts track money in and out
- Accounts have lifecycle (active/closed/dormant)
- Income is reviewed by Treasurer and approved exactly once
- Pending income excluded from balances
- Approved income updates account balance
- Audited account adjustments and transfers
- Account links are role-controlled
- Treasurer can transfer between accounts
